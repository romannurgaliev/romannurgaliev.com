Overview

Дизайн Такой is a podcast about the design of products that change how we live. Nikita Lakeev and I started it in Novosibirsk and co-hosted all 61 episodes, from design systems in December 2018 to design at FAANG companies in December 2022. Each episode is a conversation with practitioners from different fields about product strategy, design process, and the stories behind their work.
